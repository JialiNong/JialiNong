## 🧳 Travel AI skills

Agent skills that plan European trips the way a seasoned budget traveller would. They work with Claude, Cursor, Codex, ChatGPT and Gemini. The agent writes JSON and a fixed template renders it into one bilingual (中文 / English) page.

🚆 [**europe-route-finder-skill**](https://github.com/JialiNong/europe-route-finder-skill) - Finds train, bus and night-train routes that booking sites miss by expanding both ends into hub cities, then compares real fares on operator sites against aggregators

🏡 [**europe-town-stay-finder-skill**](https://github.com/JialiNong/europe-town-stay-finder-skill) - Finds stays in a small town and every village within 1 hour by public transport, compares Booking and Airbnb, and lists outdoor sports and komoot hikes nearby

🎨 [**europe-city-culture-planner-skill**](https://github.com/JialiNong/europe-city-culture-planner-skill) - Plans culture-first city days from official tourism sites: current exhibitions, markets, viewpoints, ferries and trams, free walking tours, concerts and stand-up
